# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want WHELO to be a place where anyone can contribute without harassment,
regardless of experience, identity or background.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct and kind. Critique code, not people.
- Assume good faith and ask before assuming intent.
- Keep discussions on topic and reproducible.
- Respect maintainers' time: search existing issues before opening a new one.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or sexualised language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions or pull requests.
- Dismissing reports of misconduct.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews
and any community channel representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the contact on
[@XNtriCT](https://github.com/XNtriCT). Reports are reviewed promptly and kept
confidential. Maintainers may warn, remove content, or ban contributors whose
behaviour violates this code.
